Use case: ui-mockup / stylized-concept. Create a polished landscape iPad gameplay concept board for World of the Pack, a portrait top-down 2D chibi fantasy pack tactical adventure. This is a design exploration, not a finished screenshot. Landscape 3:2 canvas. Style: richly hand-painted mobile fantasy, warm woodland greens, tan trail, restrained carved wood and brass UI, clear flat top-down battlefield with slight tilt. Composition: central portrait gameplay corridor occupies middle 50% width; left and right side helpers occupy remaining margins and should feel useful and readable. Central environment: sunlit forest path starts at bottom, visibly forks around a wooded island at upper middle; left route has berry bushes, small healing spring and an archer lookout visible behind rocks; right route is a broader ridge with two armored enemies, a fallen log and a reward chest; both reconnect toward a small warmly glowing lantern refuge at top. Keep combat clear, decoration at outside edges, four small original chibi woodland adventurers in a diamond at lower middle (swordsman leader, shield guard, green archer, blue fairy healer), two enemies in the distance. Do not copy any established franchise characters. Top center short objective text exactly 'REACH LANTERN REFUGE'. Under it a compact three-node journey marker. Bottom center four compact cards with simple portraits, no excessive gold borders, and two readable buttons 'GUARD' and 'MOONSHIFT'. Left helper has heading 'THE JOURNEY', a clean branching route diagram with 3 marked stops and final camp lantern, small heading 'NEXT THREAT', then one archer portrait with short readable phrase 'Archer ahead'. Right helper has heading 'YOUR PACK', four portrait rows with clear health bars, heading 'TACTICAL TIP', readable short phrase 'Shield the leader'. One restrained small footer at bottom outside gameplay says 'TACTICAL ADVENTURE CONCEPT'. Make all text minimal and legible. Show the destination, near-term threat, and reason to choose a route. No photorealistic device frame, no watermark, no giant central modal, no complex HUD clutter. The side helpers only explain information also available in the central gameplay; do not imply extra iPad-only powers.
