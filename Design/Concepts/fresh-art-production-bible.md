# World of the Pack — Fresh art production bible v01

## Decision

Build the game as a **bright, portrait, overhead pack adventure** with emotional warmth at its center. The art must carry the original promise — a family protecting one another — while using the current friendly top-down combat language. The old side-view, dark-survival visual brief is historical reference only; it is not the screen, camera, UI, or cast target for production.

The seven-hero paired human/wolf roster is canonical. Do not substitute the earlier Guardian, Ranger, and Helper placeholder cast when making new work.

## What changed, and why the project feels disconnected

| Layer | What is currently established | Production ruling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original pitch | Moonlit side-view escape, ability-card tactics, three companions. | Keep its emotional stakes, shelter warmth, and hunted-world story; discard its side-view layout and spell-card UI. |
| Approved gameplay direction | Sunny top-down portrait squad combat; enemies defeated to charge reusable unit-spawn cards. | This is the playable visual foundation. Preserve its camera, clear north/south flow, friendly soldiers, blue/orange team language, and legible card energy. |
| Roster/pipeline | Seven named heroes with paired human and friendly bipedal-wolf forms. | This is the canonical cast and silhouette system for all new menu, pack, story, and environment work. |

The fresh look comes from **one warm home base, one coherent family of heroes, and several clearly different journey biomes**. It does not come from repainting every scene dark or changing the approved characters.

## Non-negotiable character locks

| Hero | Must be readable at mobile size | Relationship role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero Wolvy | Blue cape, gold paw brooch, gray fur, balanced leader silhouette. | Emotional center; checks that nobody is left behind. |
| Princess Elva | Triangular green hood, leaf tiara, leaf bow. | Observant pathfinder; quietly challenges Wolvy when caution is needed. |
| Fairy Lulu | Cream/aqua silhouette, crescent clip and wand. | The heart of the group; makes safety visible through small caring gestures. |
| Captain Bobo | Widest body, coral/honey shape, large acorn shield. | Protective big-sibling energy; anchors the group physically and emotionally. |
| Scout Pip | Smallest agile silhouette, long mint scarf, map satchel. | Curious spark; pulls the group forward, especially alongside Elva and Tilly. |
| Wizard Momo | Tall drooping moon hat, lavender stars, star wand. | Dreamy wonder; turns fear into fascination and shares quiet scenes with Lulu. |
| Tinker Tilly | Circular goggles, yellow overalls, rounded backpack. | Resourceful maker; creates the visible camp improvements and teams up with Pip. |

### Relationship staging rules

- **Wolvy + Elva:** place them on the route edge, reading the next step together; leadership is shared, not only heroic posing.
- **Lulu + Bobo:** make safety tangible — healing light near Bobo's shield, a blanket, snack, or calm rest beat.
- **Pip + Tilly:** maps, repaired lanterns, small gadgets, and discovery props; their energy should break up formal group shots.
- **Momo + Lulu:** soft moon/star magic and reassurance; use the quiet upper or rear layer of a composition.
- **Whole pack:** use a loose crescent or travelling wedge. Do not line the heroes up like disconnected card portraits except in a roster screen.

## Screen language

### Main menu — “Pack Hearth”

The main menu is the emotional promise: all seven heroes are safely together at a reusable forest refuge. It is not a battle screenshot.

- Portrait 9:16, elevated top-down camera.
- Upper safe area: `WORLD OF THE PACK` on a carved wood-and-moon plaque.
- Center: a warm campsite tableau. Wolvy is central; Elva watches the trail; Lulu is close to a companion; Bobo guards the fire edge; Pip studies a map; Momo adds a tiny star light; Tilly repairs a lantern.
- Bottom: exactly `PLAY`, `MY PACK`, `OPTIONS`. No currency, shop, or daily-pressure widgets.
- Play transitions to Story Levels and Endless; My Pack opens the roster/relationship screen.

### My Pack — “Living roster”

This screen replaces a sterile character grid. The selected hero stands in the central camp, switching human/wolf form. The other six remain in a soft semicircle around the edge, each with a short relationship cue rather than stat clutter.

- One large selected portrait/form toggle.
- Seven clear roster medallions, using each locked silhouette/accessory.
- Relationship tab: show two or three illustrated connection tokens, e.g. `Leads with Elva`, `Protected by Bobo`, `Inspires Pip`.
- Role and card function must be visually separate: character identity is not a consumable unit card.

### Story journey

Keep the existing map-to-prepare-to-battle-to-refuge structure, but replace the provisional roles in new screens with the named roster. Each chapter introduces one relationship beat and one environmental memory.

| Chapter | Biome | Relationship beat | Landmark / palette |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Stay Together | Sunny Forest Trail | Wolvy, Elva, and Pip establish the group’s route. | Peach path, leaf green, teal shade, gold sun. |
| 2 — Strange Lights | Mushroom Grove | Lulu and Momo make an unfamiliar place feel safe. | Mint/teal foliage, coral and violet mushrooms, warm sand. |
| 3 — Hold the Crossing | River Crossing | Bobo protects the team while Tilly restores the crossing. | Turquoise water, honey timber, moss green. |
| 4 — The Quiet Village | Emberlane Refuge | Pip and Tilly turn found objects into a real camp; the pack earns rest. | Dusty terracotta, faded teal, amber window lights. |
| 5 — Above the Clouds | Cloudstep Highlands | Elva and Wolvy choose the final route together; every hero is visible. | Sky cyan, lavender stone, meadow emerald, sun gold. |

### Endless mode

Endless mode is not a second art style. Start from a time-of-day variation of the selected chapter biome. As a run grows, add more pack-made signs of survival: Bobo’s shield marks, Tilly’s small devices, Momo’s star lanterns, Lulu’s cloth charms, Pip’s route markers, Elva’s leaf arrows, Wolvy’s paw crest. These are progression cues — never floor clutter in the combat corridor.

## Environment system

The battle corridor is a promise to the player: allies enter at the bottom and move north; enemies enter at the top and move south. Keep center-floor detail low contrast and keep props at the margins.

### Fresh environment studies

These are mood/progression explorations, not final tileable production backgrounds:

- [Whispering Canopy Route](../../Assets/Visuals/FreshArtDirection/whispering-canopy-route-v01.png): nighttime magical forest; use after the sunny forest, retaining the same wide central corridor.
- [Emberlane Refuge](../../Assets/Visuals/FreshArtDirection/emberlane-refuge-v01.png): safe village chapter/refuge; use for story, menu variation, and post-level rest.
- [Cloudstep Highlands](../../Assets/Visuals/FreshArtDirection/cloudstep-highlands-v01.png): a hopeful highland/sanctuary approach; use as a major mood lift after darker chapters.

For final implementation, split every biome into ground, edge props, foreground, and VFX layers. Do not ship these full illustrations as a single scrolling tile.

## Visual production backlog

### First pass — art direction lock

1. Main-menu concept with the **existing seven hero designs** composited in Pack Hearth.
2. My Pack / relationship concept with a human-wolf toggle and seven readable medallions.
3. Story-map refresh: named heroes replace Guardian/Ranger/Helper everywhere.
4. One battle mockup using three named hero cards with independently charging meters and a clearly readable `SPAWN` / `CHARGING` state.

### Second pass — reusable art

1. Camp/Hearth background layers and six relationship prop sets: map, lantern, shield rest, moon charm, repair bench, shared meal.
2. Biome layer kits for Mushroom Grove, River Crossing, Emberlane, and Cloudstep.
3. Seven small card portraits, roster medallions, and relationship-token icons built from the approved sheets.
4. Shelter/refuge interactions with two-hero and full-pack variants.

### Validation checklist

- Every character remains identifiable by silhouette/accessory at card size and north-facing gameplay size.
- Human and wolf forms retain eye color, costume color, emblem, and signature accessory.
- Menu/tableau hero count is seven; gameplay squad count can grow through repeat spawns.
- Only ready cards say `SPAWN`; cards below their own threshold say `CHARGING`.
- No environment hides actors or narrows the corridor without a tested mechanic.
- The player can distinguish a home/refuge screen from a battle screen in one second.

## Explicitly avoid

- Returning to a side-view battlefield, spell-card deck, dark-horror palette, or realistic wolves.
- Creating a new cast or changing the seven locked signature accessories.
- Treating all heroes as equally posed, disconnected mascot portraits in story scenes.
- Adding shops, currencies, loot clutter, joystick controls, or extra HUD meters to make screens feel busy.
- Using generated full-screen concept images directly as production backgrounds without layer/sprite preparation.
